<objective>
Phase 13 hot-fix: r2 6.0.5's `cfg.sandbox` engages at argv-evaluation time, which means
`-e cfg.sandbox=true` in argv causes `r_core_file_open` to refuse to open the binary
ITSELF (in-container log.level=4 probe: "ERROR: Cannot open '/bin/ls'"). Move the
sandbox latch from argv to the FIRST line of the post-spawn stdin init batch so the
binary opens unsandboxed, then sandbox engages BEFORE any user-controlled r2 command.

Purpose: Make `open_r2_session` actually work against the bundled Kali r2 6.0.5 while
preserving the Phase 13 security boundary (sandbox is latched BEFORE any
user-controlled command). Without this fix, every `open_r2_session` call against a
real binary fails at spawn time and the entire Phase 13 sandbox boundary is
effectively bypassed (no sessions can be created, so nothing is sandboxed).

Output: r2.py argv no longer carries `cfg.sandbox=true`; the post-spawn stdin batch
now starts with `e cfg.sandbox=true`. test_r2_argv.py rewritten to assert the new
contract. 13-CONTEXT.md amended (D-07 reframed, D-21 added). 13-VERIFICATION.md
re-verification appended and top-line flipped to PASS.
